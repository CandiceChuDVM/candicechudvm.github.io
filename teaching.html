Teaching Philosophy

Core Belief
Technology as an equalizer

AI and digital tools can democratize access to high-quality veterinary education — making expert-level knowledge available to learners regardless of institutional resources or geography.

Approach
Hands-on, evidence-based learning

Students learn best by doing. Courses are built around real clinical cases, live AI tool demonstrations, and critical evaluation exercises — not passive lectures.

Responsibility
Critical AI literacy

Transformative potential must be paired with critical thinking. Students learn to recognize AI limitations — hallucination, bias, and ethical considerations — alongside practical applications.

Mission
Train next-generation veterinarians

Veterinary professionals who can confidently evaluate, communicate about, and responsibly implement AI tools — no programming background required.
