Candice P. Chu, DVM, PhD, DACVP

Advancing Veterinary Diagnostics and Education Through Artificial Intelligence

Dr. Candice Chu is a board-certified veterinary clinical pathologist and Assistant Professor at Texas A&M University. Her work focuses on veterinary artificial intelligence, digital cytology, diagnostic AI, large language models, and AI education. She develops and evaluates AI methods for veterinary diagnostics while preparing veterinary students and professionals to use emerging technologies responsibly and effectively.
